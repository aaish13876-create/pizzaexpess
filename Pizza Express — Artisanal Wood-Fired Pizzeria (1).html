How we do it

The secret is, there isn't one.

A good pizza comes down to a few boring things done properly. We just happen to be stubborn about them.

01

Sourdough, 48 hours

The dough gets a two-day nap before it ever meets the oven. Comes out lighter, blistered, a little bit smug.

02

Tomatoes from Campania

Grown in volcanic soil near Naples, crushed by hand, and mostly left alone. That’s the trick.

03

A proper wood oven

Four-hundred-and-fifty degrees, about sixty seconds a pizza. There’s no shortcut that beats fire.
